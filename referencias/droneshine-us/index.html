Serving all of Florida

A smarter way to maintain Florida buildings.

Florida’s heat, humidity and daily summer storms mean algae, mold and mildew grow back on stucco, tile and glass within a year or two of any cleaning. Drone Shine was built for exactly this climate: scheduled, drone-powered soft washing that keeps commercial facades, roofs, windows, solar arrays, billboards and industrial structures looking new — from downtown Orlando high-rises and Tampa Bay hotels to Miami condominiums, Jacksonville hospitals and solar farms across Central Florida.

Every surface gets a precise, controlled clean with EPA-compliant, biodegradable solutions and purified water — safe for your tenants, your landscaping and the environment. And because our aircraft carries a camera, every project ships with before-and-after photos of every elevation.

Property managers, HOA boards, hospitality groups and facility directors keep us on file because the paperwork is as clean as the buildings: FAA Part 107 certified pilots, full commercial insurance, chemical safety sheets on request, and documentation your insurance carrier will actually accept. One vendor, one certificate of insurance, and a crew that never leaves the ground.
