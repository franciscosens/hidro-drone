About Lucid Bots

Lucid Bots is a Charlotte, North Carolina-based robotics company that manufactures autonomous cleaning drones for commercial building maintenance. The company’s flagship product, the Sherpa cleaning drone, is the most widely deployed commercial cleaning drone in North America, with over 400 active operators across more than 40 states as of 2026. Operators have collectively generated more than $75 million in revenue from drone cleaning services. The company was founded in 2018, employs approximately 70 people, and is venture-backed through Series B funding.

Sherpa® Drone

The purpose-built cleaning and window washing drone makes tough exterior cleaning and spraying jobs a breeze. The Sherpa Drone takes efficiency, safety, and profitability to the next level.
