Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide roof cleaning services in Surrey and Hampshire?

Yes, we provide professional roof cleaning and moss removal services across Surrey and Hampshire. We use safe manual and soft wash methods to remove moss, algae, and debris while protecting your roof tiles and extending the lifespan of your roof.

Do you offer commercial drone cleaning services?

Yes. We provide commercial drone cleaning solutions for large buildings, industrial sites, and commercial properties across Surrey and Hampshire. This includes high-level roof cleaning, façade cleaning, and inspections that would normally require expensive access equipment.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Gutters should typically be cleaned at least once per year. Properties surrounded by trees may require more frequent gutter clearing to prevent blockages, leaks, and water damage.

Is render cleaning safe for my property?

Yes. Our render and exterior wall cleaning services use safe, surface-appropriate methods to remove algae, staining, and pollution without damaging the render or brickwork.

What is a drone roof inspection and how does it work?

A drone roof inspection uses advanced drone technology to safely assess roofs, façades, and hard-to-reach areas without scaffolding or ladders. This allows us to carry out detailed roof surveys quickly, safely, and with minimal disruption for residential and commercial properties.

Do you provide driveway and patio pressure washing?

Yes, we offer professional driveway and patio cleaning services in Surrey and Hampshire. Our pressure washing removes moss, weeds, oil stains, and surface dirt to restore the appearance of your outdoor surfaces.

Are you fully insured for exterior cleaning and drone services?

Yes, we are fully insured and operate in line with UK safety and compliance regulations for both traditional exterior cleaning and commercial drone operations.

Do you serve both residential and commercial clients?

Absolutely. We work with homeowners, property managers, facility management companies, and commercial asset managers across Surrey and Hampshire.
