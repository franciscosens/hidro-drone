How It Works

1. Building Assessment

We evaluate your building’s structure, materials, hard-to-reach areas, fragile decorative elements, and windows. This can often be done remotely for your convenience.

2. Scheduling & Estimate

Once the assessment is complete, we’ll schedule a cleaning session and provide an estimate of the hours needed to clean your building’s façade.

3. Advanced Cleaning System

Using our specialized, patent-pending pump system, we expertly control the flow of water to clean the targeted façade. This process, perfected through years of research, is both an art and a science. All cleaning products used are safe, environmentally friendly, and free from harmful chemicals like bleach.

4. Drone-Powered Cleaning

Our U.S.-manufactured drones, connected to our proprietary pumping system, are expertly piloted from the ground by our skilled operators. With precision and care, the drones begin the revolutionary cleaning process, delivering exceptional results for your building.
