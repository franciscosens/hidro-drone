Revolutionising Aerial Cleaning

Why use Drone Clean UK

The highest cause of death and injury in the cleaning industry is falling from heights.

With the utilisation of advanced technology and the power of drones, workers no longer need to undertake tasks at heights, such as rappelling off buildings on ropes, working from cradles, leaning off aerial lifts, or scaffolding.

At Drone Clean UK we prioritise safety while ensuring exceptional results for our clients. Our cutting-edge approach signifies a shift towards a safer, quicker, and more efficient method of cleaning at heights, setting a new standard in the industry.

5 times faster than conventional cleaning which uses lifts, scaffolding, or ropes.

Drone technology revolutionises the cleaning process by efficiently covering large areas, cleaning up to 400 sqm per hour.

This impressive speed surpasses traditional methods, eliminating the need for constant repositioning of lifts or scaffolds and tedious manual descent for building cleaning.

Benefit from less disruption to business, need for costly lifts & longer lasting cleans.

Unlike manual cleaning methods, there is no need to employ large teams or involve costly and environmentally heavy lift equipment.

Using our smart cleaning solutions provides a deep clean lasting longer than conventional jetwashing, increasing the time between cleans and reducing costs.

Thanks to the efficiency of our cutting-edge systems, business disruption is reduced immensely, enabling businesses to operate seamlessly without interruption.

Precise application of our powerful cleaning solutions even in the most challenging areas.

Drones can reach places that conventional cleaning methods find difficult.

Using our highly CAA trained pilots our specially formulated biodegradable cleaning products are applied accurately everytime.

These solutions are applied at low pressure so as not to effect the buildings, high pressure can be used if really needed.

Our unique cleaning solutions not only have powerful cleaning properties but continue to clean after we are gone, due to being a deep clean this reduced algae regrowth, these are then washed down with deionised water for totally streak free windows and facades

Drone Clean UK

Drone Clean UK are leading drone cleaning in the UK.

Our cutting-edge technology revolutionises cleaning at heights, making it safer, quicker, and more efficient than ever before.

Utilising heavy-lift drone development we are the only company in the UK that can offer both low-pressure and high-pressure cleaning when needed.

At every job, we will have at least two fully qualified drone pilots, that have passed the rigorous CAA tests and requirements to be able to skillfully fly heavy-lift drones.

Our main cleaning process is non-contact softwashing using our specially formulated Biodegradable cleaning solutions, these will be adjusted on each job depending on the material and amount of contamination and dirt to clean. Once we have applied the correct solution this is left to work its magic for about 30 minutes, we then rinse off with pure heated deionised water to leave a totally clean and streak-free finish. Our solutions are not just cleaning the outside but also the root of the algae, resulting in a longer-lasting solution.

Our drone washing service is ideally suited for buildings ranging from three to fifteen storeys in height.
