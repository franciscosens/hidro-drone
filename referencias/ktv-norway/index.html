With the KTV Working Drone, we offer a wide range of services including facade cleaning, window cleaning, solar panel cleaning, cleaning of installations and infrastructure, drone inspections, and, in the near future, surface treatment and more. KTV Working Drone is the only certified company in the world offering autonomous cleaning drones.

Our technology is designed to eliminate the need for personnel to work at heights or in risky environments. By removing people from potentially dangerous tasks, we not only increase safety but also deliver faster and higher-quality results.

We work across various industries including property maintenance, oil & gas, infrastructure, and energy. Our solutions cover both cleaning and surface treatment, as well as inspections.

KTV Group has developed unique, proprietary cleaning methods such as the KTV SelfCleaner, KTV Window Metal Cleaner, and KTV Nano-Coating. These systems are engineered to avoid surface damage, especially from high-pressure washing, ensuring safe and effective maintenance of even the most sensitive materials.

The drone, developed by KTV Group, uses a non-contact cleaning approach that combines filtered water, heat, and moderate pressure, free from chemicals or mechanical abrasion.The drone can also apply KTV SelfCleaner, a specialized facade cleaning agent developed and used by KTV Group since 2015.

Additionally, our patented roof safety system anchors the drone securely to the building’s roof, eliminating safety risks to people and traffic in and around the work area.

Since 1992, KTV have been working in the maintenance industry, specializing in cleaning and surface treatment services. Our tailored methods are designed to effectively address and prevent damage to buildings during maintenance procedures.

Introducing the KTV Care agreement
With the KTV Care agreement, we take full responsibility for ensuring that facades and windows remain clean through a long-term maintenance plan. In addition, every three years an inspection is carried out along with a condition report.

Clients receive access to the Bluetag quality system portal, where they can easily view the full history of completed work and inspection reports. This agreement provides full oversight of external damage and future maintenance needs.

All reports are prepared by structural engineers and focus on building damage, thermal leaks, cracks, water ingress, and general needs for cleaning or surface treatment.

By combining cleaning and inspection, costs are significantly reduced for the client while ensuring complete control over the building’s maintenance.

Why choose KTV Care?
There is no building in the world that doesn’t require cleaning or ongoing maintenance. The only question is how you choose to manage it. Neglecting regular cleaning and inspections will inevitably lead to increased long-term costs.

Remember: over a 100-year period, maintenance costs for a building are often far higher than the original construction cost.

Cleaning, surface treatment, and inspection in hazardous environments

Our drone-based cleaning, surface treatment and inspection services eliminate the need for workers to operate in hazardous environments. This drastically reduces or eliminates the cost and complexity associated with scaffolding and rope access.

With expertise in cleaning, surface treatment and inspections services for industrial and offshore environments, we’ve developed sustainable and cost-effective solutions tailored for the oil and gas sector. These facilities consist primarily of metallic structures, which demand careful cleaning to preserve protective coatings and extend lifespan.

The KTV SelfCleaner is a unique, in-house developed cleaning system designed to clean effectively without causing surface damage, ensuring long-term protection of critical assets.

We take privacy and data protection seriously

We are committed to ensuring the highest standards of privacy and data protection.

We are committed to ensuring the highest standards of privacy and data protection.

The drones used during operations are equipped with high-resolution RGB cameras, which are used by the pilot for navigation purposes. The recording function is disabled, meaning no video or images are stored during cleaning operations with the drone.

For certain assignments, we may use a mini drone to film the cleaning drone in action. This is done for marketing purposes or to document the work being performed. Any such filming is agreed upon with the client in advance. In these cases, the Data Processing Agreement applies. All collected data is handled exclusively by KTV Working Drone, which is the authorized data processor.

The data processor is responsible at all times for ensuring that collected data and personal information are stored and handled in accordance with the applicable Data Processing Agreement and relevant legislation.

If individuals, sensitive personal information, or other confidential content are captured as part of the data collection, the data processor is required to censor such information before any further use of the data. The operating pilot(s) must proactively notify third parties if the collected data contains information that could violate personal privacy. The pilot(s) must also, to the greatest extent possible, ensure that data collection is carried out in a manner that minimizes the risk of capturing individuals, sensitive personal information, or other confidential content in the collected imagery.
