Why Choose SkyWash Drones?

  • Safer Cleaning at Height: Our cleaning drones eliminate the hazards associated with people physically working at height to reduce risk and limit your exposure, giving you peace of mind.
  • Faster and More Efficient: Our innovative drone cleaning method powers through jobs five times faster so you can easily schedule work and keep control of operational costs.
  • Minimal Disruption: We operate a seamless cleaning process without the need for scaffolding or other height access equipment so you can get on with your daily operations with minimal disruption.
  • Easily Clean Hard-to-Reach Areas: Our drones can easily clean hard-to-reach elevations that lack the ground space needed for equipment like sky lifts so you can finally clean those neglected areas without those sky-high access costs.
  • Eco-Friendly Solutions: Our simple access requirements and eco-friendly cleaning methods help you control your carbon footprint without compromising on results.

High-Rise Window and Facade Cleaning

SkyWash Drones provides a variety of exterior cleaning options tailored to your building’s requirements.


  • Drone Window Cleaning: Our innovative cleaning drones will bring back the sparkle to your windows.
  • Drone Facade Cleaning: Keep your facade in tip-top condition using our soft-washing solutions. We clean all types of facade safely while preserving the integrity of your building’s fabric.
  • Solar Panel Cleaning: Gentle and effective cleaning to restore optimal function and protect your commercial solar panels from unseen contaminants. Unlike solar robots, our drone cleaning method is non-contact and does not harm the panel surface but still cleans effectively (but much faster) to keep your solar array or solar farm in optimal condition.
  • Soft Washing: Gentle yet thorough cleaning that does not damage your building’s surface materials, using eco-friendly products.
  • Power Washing: High-pressure cleaning for tough grime and stains on surfaces that can handle it.
