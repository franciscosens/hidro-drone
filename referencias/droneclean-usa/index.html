Give your Property a Better clean

Without the Risk

At Drone Clean USA, we’re redefining exterior cleaning with advanced drone and robotic technology. Our Charleston-based, veteran-owned team provides safe, non-invasive cleaning for homes, businesses, and high-rise buildings across the U.S. We reach up to 250 feet without ladders or scaffolding—ensuring a thorough, efficient clean without putting people or property at risk.

A Mission That Goes Beyond Clean

At Drone Clean USA, we believe excellence starts from within. As a veteran-owned company rooted in Christian values, we are guided by integrity, humility, and purpose in every service we provide. Inspired by scriptures like Colossians 3:23—"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord"—we strive to honor both God and our clients by delivering quality work with honesty and care.

FAA Licensed Drone Pilots

Every cleaning technician at Drone Clean USA is a fully licensed FAA drone pilot with a Remote Pilot Certificate and sUAS rating. Our team is trained to the highest safety standards and undergoes comprehensive background checks. For added transparency, all personnel carry QR-verifiable ID badges, and clients receive an email introduction the night before service for peace of mind.

Trusted Service Provider

Drone Clean USA is a proud partner with the Seal of Security & Confidence, verifying our commitment to safety, accountability, and professionalism. Our team members are insured, vetted, and third-party verified. When you see our badge, you know you're working with a provider you can trust.

Safer & More Efficient Cleaning

Our AI-enhanced drones eliminate the risks of traditional pressure washing. No climbing on rooftops. No shaky ladders. Just fast, accurate, and damage-free exterior cleaning for residential and commercial properties. We reduce liability, protect your structures, and achieve longer-lasting results—all while using less water than conventional methods.

Proudly Veteran Owned

As a veteran-founded and operated company, we hold ourselves to a higher standard. We value integrity, attention to detail, and respect for your property. Every project is approached with a mission-first mindset and a commitment to excellence.
